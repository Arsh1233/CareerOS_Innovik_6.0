# Vision Statement

CareerOS is an Agentic AI-powered Career Operating System designed to bridge the gap between education and employment.

Unlike traditional career platforms that focus on only one aspect of the journey (learning, networking, or job searching), CareerOS continuously monitors, predicts, guides, and accelerates a student's career growth using multiple AI agents.

The platform serves three stakeholders:

1. Students
2. Colleges
3. Recruiters

All stakeholders operate within a unified AI-powered ecosystem.

---

# Why CareerOS Exists

Today's career ecosystem is fragmented.

Students use:

* LinkedIn for networking
* Coursera for learning
* Naukri for jobs
* ChatGPT for guidance
* LeetCode for interview preparation

These platforms do not communicate with each other.

As a result:

* Students do not know what skills to learn
* Colleges cannot accurately track employability
* Recruiters struggle to identify suitable candidates

CareerOS solves this by becoming the central intelligence layer connecting all stakeholders.
